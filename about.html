About us

Electrify Christchurch is a volunteer-powered charitable trust.

Our mission

Helping Christchurch residents and businesses electrify their buildings and transport, powered by renewables.

Who we work with

We’re part of the wider Rewiring Aotearoa community network, and we collaborate with relevant local community organisations across Christchurch and the greater region.
