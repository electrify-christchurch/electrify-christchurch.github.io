Going electric pays off for Christchurch

Switching from petrol and gas to electric saves households money, and when lots of homes switch, the whole city benefits.

For one household

$7,600a year off the energy bills of an average home that switches from petrol and gas to all-electric, with solar and a battery
$3,700a year better off even after paying for the upgrades

For the whole city

$1.1 billiona year saved on energy bills across Christchurch
740,000tonnes less climate pollution (CO₂e) every year
5,100new local jobs, about one for every 80 residents

Based on Rewiring Aotearoa’s Christchurch regional report. Household figures are for a home currently using LPG appliances and petrol cars. City figures assume homes and vehicles switch to electric, with solar and batteries on most homes.
